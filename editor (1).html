How to update your online game

  1. Download the JSON file or full backup.
  2. Open your GitHub repository on your phone and open its content folder.
  3. Replace challenges.json and/or game-settings.json with the downloaded files.
  4. Commit changes and wait for your hosting service to redeploy.

Changes in this editor are saved locally in this browser only. They do not publish themselves to your live game. The server currently loads its content at startup, so redeploy/restart after changing these files.
